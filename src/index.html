      Check before you book. Built-in timetables were copied from ConfirmTkt and RailMitra in October 2026; live timetables come from erail.in. These are unofficial third-party sources. Confirm every train and seat on IRCTC or NTES (enquiry.indianrail.gov.in) before paying.

      • Nearby stations use straight-line distance × 1.3 as a road estimate. Road time is that distance divided by your road speed. Station list and coordinates: datameet/railways (CC0).
      • Changing between stations in the same city (Howrah ↔ Santragachi or Dankuni, Ranchi ↔ Hatia, Kharagpur ↔ Hijli, Jasidih ↔ Deoghar, SMVT ↔ KR Puram) adds a cab transfer of 20–75 min on top of your connection buffer.
      • Each route uses the latest run of its final train that lands in your window, and the latest earlier trains that still connect.
      • Reservations open 60 days before a train leaves its origin station. Your settings are saved in this browser only. The page contacts erail.in (live trains) and Google Fonts (typefaces).
